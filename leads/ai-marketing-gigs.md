### 1. Upwork (best fit)
- **How it works:** You bid on jobs with Connects ($0.15 each; 10 free a month). Clients can also invite you or buy fixed-price Project Catalog offers.
- **Fees:** 0–15% per contract, shown before you send a proposal. Skip Freelancer Plus ($19.99/month, reported) for now.
- **AI rules:** Allowed unless the client restricts it; breaking a "no AI" rule counts as misleading conduct. Upwork recommends telling clients up front. To keep your work out of Upwork's AI training, turn it off under Settings > AI preferences.
- **Prices seen:** AI UGC freelancers charge about $7–$30/hr. Budgets run from $50 to $500 fixed, plus one $3,600 job for 36 avatar videos.
- **Open these first** (seen in search only; dates are estimates):
  - [Higgsfield AI Video and Ad Creative Specialist: Master Prompter (Long Term)](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Higgsfield-Video-and-Creative-Specialist-Master-Prompter-for-Performance-Ads-Long-Term_~022112563545371192240/): ~Oct 20, under 30 hrs/week, 6+ months. ### 2. Fiverr
- **How it works:** Clients find your gig and order a package. Fiverr matches some "Briefs" to sellers; you can't browse them. Fiverr Pro comes later, once you have client work.
- **Fees:** Fiverr keeps 20%, tips included. Buyers pay about 5.5% on top.
- **AI rules:** Allowed. If a client says "no AI", honor it. Misrepresenting AI use can mean a full refund and suspension. No deepfakes, and don't sell the same AI clip to several buyers.
- **Prices seen:** AI UGC gigs start at $5–$200, mostly $10–$25.
- **Setup:** Publish niche gigs, "Bilingual (English/Spanish) AI UGC ads" and "AI promo reels for restaurants and food trucks", with 3 packages each. The restaurant gig can mirror your $49 Event Pack and $79 Launch Pack. State usage rights and revisions, and reply fast.

### 3. Google Business Profile (free, local clients)
- **How it works:** Owners searching "video production Kissimmee" or similar see your profile and contact you.
- **Rules:** You must meet customers in person (e.g. visiting a restaurant to plan or film); online-only work doesn't qualify. Set up as a service-area business with the address hidden. Google needs a real address to verify you but won't show it. No P.O. boxes or virtual offices here. Name: "elier.visuals", no extra keywords.
- **Setup:** Create it at [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business/). Pick a video production or marketing category, choose "I deliver to customers", and add Davenport, Four Corners, Kissimmee, Celebration, Haines City, Lakeland, Orlando and Winter Haven. Verify (maybe by video). Add services, prices, both languages and labeled samples. Ask every happy client for a review.

### 4. Contra (free portfolio and payments)
- **How it works:** Clients browse portfolios and hire you directly. There's a job feed; check it weekly.
- **Fees:** No commission for you. On the free plan the client pays $15 (under $500) or $29 ($500 and up) per payment, plus card or ACH fees. Skip Pro ($29/month, reported) for now.
- **Setup:** Free profile as elier.visuals (UGC Creator / Video). Post each sneaker video as a project with a note on the hook and tools. These become your application links. [Contra UGC creators](https://contra.com/discover?roles=UGC+Creator&view=people)

### 5. Thumbtack (paid local leads)
- Customers post requests, and several pros get each one. Leads cost about $30 and up (pros report $138 for a lead on a $450 job; unofficial). Only about 5 five-star social media consultants list in Kissimmee.
- Choose Social Media Marketing, not Video Production (which draws wedding requests). Cap spending at $50–$100 a week, say up front your videos are AI-made, and track cost per booked job. [Kissimmee page](https://www.thumbtack.com/fl/kissimmee/social-media-marketing)

### 6. Nextdoor Business Page (free)
- Neighbors find you through recommendations and "Local Faves". Free page and posts. Before publishing, check whether it can run without a public street address. [Claim the page](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/getting-started/business-page)

### 7. Runway DIFFUSE (free AI-video showcase)
- Brands and studios browse AI video creators. Jobs is "coming soon". Post your best 3–5 AI ads, labeled. [diffuse.runway.com](https://diffuse.runway.com)

### 8. Bark (paid local leads, try last)
- You spend credits ($2.35 each) to contact a buyer, and about 5 pros get each lead. Credits expire after 3 months and aren't refundable. Buy the smallest pack only after Upwork, Fiverr and Google are running, and answer only leads with a budget. [bark.com](https://www.bark.com)
