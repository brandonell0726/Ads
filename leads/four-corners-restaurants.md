## Sending tips

- **Send at most about 50 emails a day** from one inbox. More than that teaches spam filters to flag your address.
- **Keep every email personal.** Copying the same text to everyone is what gets flagged as spam.
- **Every email needs the "business pitch" line, your mailing address and the opt-out line.** US law (CAN-SPAM) requires them.
- **Follow up once, after 4–5 days at most, then stop.** Check for replies first and skip anyone who already answered.
- **"No thanks" is final.** Add them to a do-not-contact list and remove them within 10 business days at the latest. Same day is better.
- **The sample is for that business only.** Don't post it or put it in your portfolio without their written OK.
