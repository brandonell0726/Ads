## Sending tips

- **Send at most about 50 emails a day** from one inbox, and keep every email personal.
- **Every email needs the "business pitch" line, your mailing address and the opt-out line.** US law (CAN-SPAM) requires them.
- **Follow up once, after 4–5 days at most, then stop.** "No thanks" is final: add them to a do-not-contact list.
- **If your samples use AI-generated people (for example, Higgsfield UGC):** tell the brand they're AI-made. They must never be posted as real customer reviews. The FTC bans fake testimonials.
- **A sample made for a brand is only for that brand.** Don't post it or put it in your portfolio without their written OK.
