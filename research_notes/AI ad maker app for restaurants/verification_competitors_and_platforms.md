# Verification: competitor and platform claims (checked 2026-11-04)

Adversarial fact-check of `competitors_general_ai_ad_tools.md` (CG below) and the platform-threat parts of `revenue_models_and_gtm.md` (RG below). The five original notes files were not edited.

**Method and limits**
- 27 WebSearch queries and 6 WebFetch attempts. One fetch, higgsfield.ai/pricing, returned no content.
- Pages read in full: Arcads' seed press release, the UsagePricing Creatify change log for 2026-10-15, RelevantAudience's write-up of Meta's Advertising Week 2026 announcements, Google's Pomelli "Get started" help page, and Google's 2026-05-19 Pomelli blog post.
- Every other verdict rests on search-result snippets from the sources listed. A verdict of CONFIRMED means at least one primary source, or two or more independent secondary sources, agree with the note. It does not mean the page was read in full.
- I did not open the Higgsfield billing widget in this session's connector to check plan prices, because it is a checkout UI. That source was deliberately skipped.

Verdict key:
- **CONFIRMED**: the note is right.
- **CORRECTED**: the note is wrong or out of date, and the right figure is given.
- **CONFLICTING**: good sources disagree.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no support found, or only weak support.

## Most important corrections

Ordered by how much they would change Brandon's decisions.

1. **Pomelli is a stronger free threat than the notes say, and it is live in the US.** Google's own help page confirms it is free "currently" with a cap of "several hundred image and video generations". Direct Instagram publishing went live on 2026-06-18, rolling out gradually. On 2026-05-19 Google added a Pomelli Agent, Brand Books and a **website builder**, so a food truck without a website can still use it. It reached Europe in April 2026. Only the Animate (video) launch date is unconfirmed. This strengthens the case for selling done-for-you outcomes, Spanish-language local work and food-specific styles instead of plain generation.
2. **Higgsfield is bigger than the notes state, and is leaning into the same customers.** It claims a run rate above $1B as of 2026-09-24, up from $700M in August, with business-contract revenue up 10x since June, driven by DTC brands making AI ads. Higgsfield is both Brandon's supplier and a direct competitor in "product → ad", which raises the supplier risk the notes already describe.
3. **Creatify's prices went down, not up.** On 2026-10-15 it replaced the $39/$99 tiers with Standard $15, Pro $55, Max $95 and Business $195. The notes' "2026 saw price rises" inference, and its view of vendor margin pressure, need rewriting. Entry price anchors for self-serve AI ad video are now around $15, which pushes Brandon's pricing toward value per finished pack rather than a cheap seat.
4. **Meta's liability terms take effect on 2026-11-20, 16 days from now; the Oct 30 date is superseded.** Advertisers are responsible for what Meta's AI creates or changes, even when an enhancement was switched on by default. They also must not remove AI watermarks or authenticity metadata. Brandon's app should keep AI metadata intact. It should also tell restaurant customers to check Advantage+ "creative enhancements" before boosting his ads.
5. **There is no evidence that Meta's "fully automated ads by end of 2026" has shipped.** Only pieces have shipped: static-to-video GA (2026-10-06), catalog video in beta, a gradual Ads Creative Studio rollout and assistant actions in test. The report should not present full automation as delivered.
6. **"Free" platform tools are partly gated.** Most Canva Grow AI ad features need a paid plan. TikTok's newest model (Seedance 2.5) is limited to select paid advertisers. Amazon's "unlimited" claim is unsupported, and the tool is irrelevant to restaurants anyway.
7. **Arcads figures need softer wording.** The press release gives a team of seven, not eight. "$15M ARR in April 2026" is a Latka profile field, not a confirmed ARR. The Creator price ($220) and the whole Arcads price list come from a competitor's blog, and Arcads publishes no prices.
8. **Higgsfield plan prices conflict.** Only Ultra at $129/mo billed monthly with 3,000 credits is consistent across sources. Brandon should read his own billing page before building cost models.
9. **Cuttable's round is A$20M, not US$20M.** That is roughly US$13M; the conversion is my estimate, not a sourced figure.
