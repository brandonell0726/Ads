# Verification: market, benchmark and case-study claims (checked 2026-11-04)

This is an adversarial fact-check of three notes files. The original notes were not edited. File abbreviations used below:
- **RM**: `restaurant_market_and_food_tools.md`
- **RG**: `revenue_models_and_gtm.md`
- **AF**: `ad_formats_and_algorithm.md`

**Method and limits**
- I ran 28 WebSearch queries and made 23 WebFetch attempts. Four fetches failed: IBISWorld returned 405 twice, and Popmenu and Restaurant Business returned 403.
- Three primary documents were downloaded and read in full as extracted text:
  - the NRA *2026 State of the Restaurant Industry* report PDF (the WTOP-hosted copy)
  - the NRA *Restaurant Owner Demographics* data brief (Apr 2026)
  - the NRA Florida state fact sheet
- These pages were also read directly, through a fetch summary:
  - DoorDash's 2026-05-04 newsroom post
  - Toast's 2026-05-05 IQ Grow release (CloudFront mirror of pos.toasttab.com)
  - the Owner.com, Marqii, FoodShot and MenuPhotoAI pricing pages
  - ChartMogul's AI churn report and RevenueCat's AI retention study
  - Meta's Nov 2024 creative-diversification post
  - Motion/Runneth's 2026 benchmark pages (main page, methodology, top visual formats, hit-rate context)
  - the Gallup poll page
  - a mirror copy of Lenny's free-to-paid article
- Every other verdict rests on search-result snippets.
- I did not re-check claims outside the priority list, such as the Toast and Popmenu vendor surveys, Square or Clover terms, the TikTok/Meta specs in AF Q4, and the legal claims.

**Verdict key**
- **CONFIRMED**: a primary source, or two or more independent secondary sources, agree with the note.
- **CORRECTED**: the note is wrong or out of date. The right figure is given.
- **CONFLICTING**: good sources disagree.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no support found, or only weak support.

## Most important corrections
These are ordered by how much they would change Brandon's decisions.

1. **There are far fewer food trucks with paid staff than the notes imply.** IBISWorld's modelled 88–92K "food truck businesses" sits beside a Census count of only **12,487 employer mobile-food establishments (2023)**, and that Census category also includes carts and catering trucks. Most "trucks" are one-person operations with little cash. A self-serve subscription aimed at food trucks has a much smaller paying base than 90K. Restaurants (48,907 in Florida) are the bigger market. Verify the Census figure on data.census.gov before using it.
2. **The cheap photo-tool tier isn't $9–$29/mo. It's about $9–$99/mo.** MenuPhotoAI charges $39–$89/mo and $49–$119 per pack, and FoodShot charges $15–$99/mo. A $29–$79/mo price would sit *inside* the existing food-photo price band, not above it. RM Q7's "above the commodity tier" positioning needs rethinking. Differentiation has to come from video, ad styles and Spanish, not from price.
3. **Owner.com is now much bigger and no longer restaurant-only.** It raised a $240M Series D at $2.3B in Aug 2026, reports ARR above $100M and is expanding to local businesses generally. RG §6's "vertical focus as a defence" example is weaker, and a well-funded $249–$499/mo incumbent is moving toward Brandon's other customers too. The Sacra $81M and $10K ACV figures are estimates, and the ACV rise was 2024→2025.
4. **The RG §2 LTV:CAC maths mixes data sources.** It divides Meta cost per trial by RevenueCat's *mobile* 8.5% trial conversion and uses ChartMogul's small sub-$50 AI-native bucket (part of about 200 companies). The direction (cheap self-serve is hard to fund with paid ads) still holds, but the 0.2–1.2 range shouldn't be stated as fact. RevenueCat's retention figures are averages, not medians, and come from consumer mobile apps.
5. **Chili's isn't proof that cheese-pull video drives sales.** The share rose from 11% (company, Oct 2024) to 14–15% (2025), and the credit to social media is the CMO's own claim. Use it as an illustration, not as evidence for the "crave video" style.
6. **Motion's benchmarks are e-commerce, Meta-only and BFCM-inflated, and the tier hit rates are 4.0–8.8%, not 3.8–8.2%.** The offer-first banner's 8.6% is "likely elevated relative to steady-state" in Motion's own words. That's fine as a reason to include an offer style, but not as a predicted hit rate for restaurant ads.
7. **Meta's "20+ diversified ads" is from Nov 2024 and applies to Advantage+ shopping/sales campaigns.** Don't build the product, or the pitch, around a 20-ad requirement for a single-location restaurant.
8. **NYU/Emory's 31.5% label penalty comes from one beauty retailer** (about 106K impressions, explicit label on display ads). It's a warning sign, not a restaurant-ad estimate. It doesn't measure Meta's hidden "AI info" tag.
9. **Zenchef's door-to-door numbers date from 2012–2013 in France**, and the 35% close rate wasn't confirmed. Use them as an anecdote, not a funnel benchmark.
10. **Popmenu's AI Marketing price is unpublished.** "$300/mo add-on + $1,300 onboarding" rests on one third-party blog. Base tiers conflict across sources ($149 to $179 entry).
11. **Smaller framing fixes:**
    - The NRA's 78% is "social media is a good way to discover restaurants" (an attitude), not measured discovery.
    - Florida's restaurant firms are 34% Hispanic-owned, but Florida's whole private sector is 38%, so the bilingual case is Florida-wide, not restaurant-specific.
    - DoorDash's free Video Library is for "eligible" merchants only.
    - Toast's +8% is GMV in a 144-restaurant vendor beta.
    - Marqii's tiers are now $90 / $145 / $180 / $250, and social management is only in the $180 and $250 tiers.
    - Datassential's 0.9% first-year failure rate is a methodological outlier. Its five-year rate is about 25–33%.
