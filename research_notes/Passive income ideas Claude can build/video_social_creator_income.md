# Mostly-automated social and video income streams (faceless YouTube, TikTok, Meta, Pinterest, Spanish-language) as of November 2026

Research date: 2026-11-04. Every number carries the date of its source. Official pages that loaded are marked (primary). Most RPM, earnings and commission figures online come from tool-vendor or SEO blogs, and these are marked (vendor) or (secondary). Official TikTok Help and Creator Academy pages did not render through the fetch tool, so the TikTok eligibility details below are secondary.

## 1. YouTube Partner Program rules, the "inauthentic content" policy, enforcement against AI channels, and what still gets monetized

### Takeaway
AI tools are allowed on YouTube. What gets demonetized or terminated is templated, mass-produced, low-variation content, such as AI slideshows, near-identical narrated stories or fake trailers, and enforcement covers the whole channel. Separately, from 1 Feb 2027 YouTube doubles the entry bar for new channels (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views). Channels that don't keep 10M Shorts views every rolling 90 days will stop earning from the Shorts pool. Small, high-volume AI Shorts channels are now a poor bet.

### Cited Findings
**Current requirements (primary, YouTube Help, read 2026-11-04)**
- YPP entry: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers plus 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. Shorts-feed watch hours don't count toward the 4,000 hours. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)
- Expanded (lower) tier, unchanged for 2027: 500 subscribers plus either 3,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. It unlocks fan funding, Creator Partnerships and Shopping, but not ad revenue. — [PPC Land, 12 Aug 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)
- Since 31 Mar 2025, a Shorts view counts from the start of playback. YouTube confirmed on 12 Oct 2025 that Shorts watch hours don't count toward the long-form threshold. — [PPC Land, 12 Aug 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)

**Changes announced 10 Aug 2026, effective 1 Feb 2027 (secondary, citing YouTube's Help Center and community post)**
- New applicants will need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days (up from 4,000), or 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days (up from 10M). Existing partners keep their YPP status. — [PPC Land, 12 Aug 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/); [NewscastStudio, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-monetization-changes-2027/); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150324/youtube-partner-program-minimum-qualified-public-watch-hours-shorts-views)
- Shorts Creator Pool floor: a channel must hold 10M qualified Shorts views over a rolling 90-day window, checked monthly, to get any share of pooled Shorts ad and Premium revenue. Below that, Shorts pool pay stops but the channel stays in YPP. Long-form ads, fan funding, Shopping affiliate and brand deals are unaffected. YouTube: "you will not be removed from YPP and this will not impact other YPP earnings." — [PPC Land, 12 Aug 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)
- Ads targeted to 5 or fewer channels will pay creators 45% of that placement directly. YouTube also promised unspecified Shopping bonuses, production credits and "cultural trend" boosts for channels below the floor, with no criteria or dates published yet. Creators must accept the new monetization modules by 31 Jan 2027. — [PPC Land, 12 Aug 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)
- YouTube cited 200B+ daily Shorts views and says it expects to pay creators more in 2027 than in 2026. — [PPC Land, 12 Aug 2026](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)

**Inauthentic content policy (primary)**
- 15 Jul 2025: "We're renaming this policy from 'repetitious content' to 'inauthentic content'." The policy covers content that is "repetitive or mass-produced." — [YouTube Help: channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Not monetizable: "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative." Also not monetizable: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production," and videos where characters repeatedly face the same situation with the same outcome. Allowed: the same intro and outro when "the bulk of your content is different," and series with distinct storylines. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Reused content: "Content that exclusively features readings of other materials you did not originally create" is not monetizable, nor are compilations of other artists' songs, even with permission. Critical reviews and reaction videos with real commentary are allowed. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- YouTube called it "a minor update to our longstanding guideline." It said such content "was never eligible for monetization." It also warned that "if you have videos that violate our guidelines, monetization may be removed from your entire channel." — [PPC Land, Jul 2025](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/)

**Enforcement 2025-2026 (secondary)**
- Dec 2025: YouTube terminated Screen Culture (1.4M+ subscribers) and KH Studio (680K+), which had a combined 2M+ subscribers and over 1B views, for AI-assisted fake movie trailers. YouTube cited "spam and misleading metadata policies." Their ads had already been suspended earlier in 2025 after a Deadline investigation. — [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/tech/youtube-terminates-screen-culture-kh-studio-over-ai-fake-movie-trailers/); [Digital Music News, 26 Dec 2025](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/12/26/youtube-ai-film-trailers-takedowns/)
- Jan 2026: YouTube terminated 16 channels with a combined 35M subscribers and 4.7B lifetime views, estimated to earn about $10M a year in total, under the inauthentic content policy. — [The Next Web, 15 Jun 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage). One summary reports 11 channels fully terminated and 5 wiped of content. A Tribune headline dated 18 Apr 2026 reports "deletes 4.7B views of AI spam videos" (page blocked, so the date and details are unverified). — [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2589980/youtube-cracks-down-deletes-47b-views-of-ai-spam-videos)
- Kapwing (28 Nov 2025, data from Oct 2025) found that 104 of the first 500 Shorts shown to a new account (21%) were AI slop and 165 (33%) were "brainrot." — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)
- Mar 2026: YouTube tested a mobile pop-up that asks viewers to rate videos as AI slop on a five-point scale. YouTube auto-labels AI videos using detection signals, C2PA metadata and SynthID. It says AI-labeled videos aren't penalized in recommendations or monetization, and that the crackdown targets mass-produced, templated content. — [TNW, 15 Jun 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)
- Collateral damage claims: TNW reports that the algorithm now favors on-camera faces and that one pattern across a creator's last 30 uploads can demonetize the whole channel. Creator Doctor NOS (1.7M subscribers) says most similar faceless creators are being demonetized, and some creators are hiring on-camera hosts on Fiverr and Upwork (via The Hollywood Reporter). These are reported claims, not YouTube statements. — [TNW, 15 Jun 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)
- Higgsfield raised $80M at a $1.3B valuation in Jan 2026 and generates 4.5M videos a day. Brandon's main tool is the same one that mass-produces competing content. — [TNW, 15 Jun 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)

**AI disclosure (primary)**
- Creators must disclose realistic GenAI content that makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't, alters footage of real events or places, generates a realistic scene that didn't happen, or creates music that is the video's main focus. This is done with the "AI use" setting in Studio. No disclosure is needed for non-realistic or animated content, minor edits, AI-written scripts, titles and thumbnails, or cloning your own voice. — [YouTube Help: Disclosing use of GenAI content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- "Disclosing AI content alone won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money." Creators who consistently fail to disclose risk manual labels, removal or YPP suspension. YouTube auto-labels content made with its own GenAI tools, content carrying C2PA metadata, and content its systems detect. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Faceless channels that can still be monetized share these traits: a distinct script and point of view in each video, real research or education value, edited visuals that aren't one template, and narration that adds commentary rather than reading someone else's text. Examples include explainers, local-history or food documentaries, and tutorials. Higgsfield clips used as B-roll inside a researched, scripted video fit the policy. A pipeline that posts dozens of near-identical AI stories or slideshows a week is exactly what the policy names.
- "Run it day and night" mass posting is now the riskiest way to run a channel. Enforcement is channel-wide, and the Feb 2027 Shorts floor means small Shorts channels earn $0 from the pool even when monetized.
- New channels that apply after 1 Feb 2027 need twice the watch time. If Brandon wants long-form YPP, qualifying before then under the 4,000-hour rule is a real but probably unrealistic deadline for a channel starting in Nov 2026.

### Gaps
- YouTube's own 10 Aug 2026 Help Center article was not fetched directly. The terms come from PPC Land, NewscastStudio and Shacknews, which agree with one another.
- One SEO site (OutlierKit) claims YouTube clarified the inauthentic policy on 16 Jul 2026 into three non-monetizable categories "according to TechCrunch." I couldn't verify this, so it's excluded.
- No official count of channels demonetized under the inauthentic policy exists. The figures circulating (for example "12M channels terminated in 2026") come from low-quality summaries and are excluded.

## 2. YouTube RPMs by niche (long-form, Shorts, Spanish), views needed for $500 and $2,000 a month, case studies, failure rates

### Takeaway
Median long-form RPM in AIR Media-Tech's 274-channel dataset (updated Sep 2026) runs from about $3 to $5 in entertainment, gaming and lifestyle up to $15 to $18 in education and science. Shorts pay $0.07 to $0.20 per 1,000 views in most niches, about 3 to 14% of long-form. Earning $2,000 a month takes roughly 130K to 670K long-form views a month, or 10M to 29M Shorts views. No reliable data on faceless-channel failure rates exists. Marketplace listings show the survivors only.

### Cited Findings
- AIR Media-Tech dataset: 274 channels across 13 niches, 3,044 channel-months, data from the YouTube Analytics API, full-year medians (not Q4 only). Small tier is 10K to 100K monthly views, medium is 100K to 10M. Last updated 16 and 17 Sep 2026. — [AIR: Shorts RPM by niche](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026); [AIR: Shorts vs long-form](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- Long-form median RPM (AIR, 2026): Education and Science $18.23 for small channels and $14.97 for medium; Entertainment $4.58 for channels without Shorts and $4.99 for channels posting 0 Shorts a month; Gaming about $3.3 to $3.4; Lifestyle (medium) $3.87 to $4.88; Crafting and Handmade $3.53 to $3.68; Kids and Teens (large) $0.49. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026). A separate AIR study (Jul 2026, 300 channels, May 2025 to May 2026), as summarized in search results, puts the median Education RPM at $10.22. — [AIR data findings index](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings)
- Shorts median RPM (AIR, 2026): $0.07 to $0.20 in most niches. Music is the outlier at $0.97 to $1.48. Lifestyle is $0.16 to $0.20, Crafting $0.11, Kids $0.02 to $0.05. By viewer country: US $0.328, Australia $0.193, UK $0.166, Canada $0.165, Germany $0.163. Most niches need 11,000 to 26,000 Shorts views to earn what 1,000 long-form views earn, and Crafting needs about 34,000. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- Within-channel correlation (not proven causal): Entertainment channels' long-form RPM was 60% lower in months with Shorts, falling to $1.15 at 21+ Shorts a month. Pure long-form Entertainment channels averaged $2,979 a month against $559 for channels mixing in Shorts irregularly. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)
- YouTube publishes no official RPM-by-niche benchmarks, and most online tables are estimates with no stated method. — [Search summary of overseeros.com and others](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-rpm-by-niche)
- Spanish-language RPM (vendor estimates only): typically $1.50 to $4.00, depending on how much of the audience is in Spain or the US rather than Latin America. US Hispanic CPM $3 to $8, Mexico $1 to $3, Spain $2 to $5. — [FluxNote Spanish channel guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/spanish-language-youtube-channel-guide); [FluxNote LatAm CPM by country](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-cpm-latin-america-by-country)
- Faceless channel marketplace listings (Flippa, live listings with seller-stated numbers, not sold prices): one at $2,082 monthly profit asking about 0.8x annual profit, another at $11,671 monthly profit asking about 2.3x. One listing claims 19.6M views, $122K revenue in 8 months and a 46% margin. Another claims 11.39M views and $50.5K lifetime revenue over 2 years. — [Flippa 11976408](https://flippa.com/11976408-faceless-youtube-channel-with-19-6m-views-122k-revenue-in-8-months-46-profit-margin-and-65k-subsscalable-viral-and-primed-for-growth); [Flippa 11897782](https://flippa.com/11897782-turnkey-faceless-youtube-channel-11-39m-views-a-proven-lifetime-revenue-of-50-500-30-in-just-2-years-seize-the-opportunity-to-own-a-fully-automated-busine); [Flippa 13042432](https://flippa.com/13042432-highly-profitable-branded-faceless-youtube-channel-with-a-trained-team-already-running-it-making-it-a-mostly-passive-business-with-strong-growth-potential). The 46% margin listing shows that profitable faceless channels at this scale pay staff (writers, editors, voice), so revenue is not profit.
- Kapwing used Social Blade to estimate top AI slop channels at about $4.25M a year (Bandar Apna Dost, India, 2.07B views) and about $4.0M a year (Three Minutes Wisdom, South Korea, 2.02B views) as of Oct 2025. These are the extreme winners. — [Kapwing, 28 Nov 2025](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)
- Failure rates: the widely repeated "97% of channels fail" and "median faceless channel $4,800 a year" figures come from content-marketing blogs with no traceable dataset. One blog's "68.4% Year 1 failure rate" is attributed to Social Blade, which I couldn't confirm. — [Frameloop](https://frameloop.ai/blog/faceless-youtube-statistics-2026); [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/faceless-youtube-channel-statistics-2026/)
- A YouTube automation operator reportedly lost about $250K a month after several faceless channels were shut down over a copyright dispute. Other channels reportedly grew to millions of subscribers and then collapsed, possibly because YouTube reduced promotion of AI channels (single article, unverified). — [Vizard blog](https://vizard.ai/blog/?p=4041)

**Views needed (my arithmetic from the AIR medians above, before tax and tool costs)**
| Monthly target | Long-form at $3 RPM | Long-form at $5 RPM | Long-form at $15 RPM (education) | Shorts at $0.07 | Shorts at $0.20 | Shorts at $0.33 (US-heavy) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $500 | 167K views | 100K | 33K | 7.1M | 2.5M | 1.5M |
| $2,000 | 667K | 400K | 133K | 28.6M | 10M | 6.1M |
From 1 Feb 2027, Shorts revenue is paid only when the channel has 10M+ qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days (about 3.3M a month), so the $500 Shorts scenarios at low RPM sit right at the cut-off.

### Inferences
- For a faceless project, long-form education or explainer content in a niche that pays $10+ is the only YouTube setup where $500 to $2,000 a month is plausible at view counts a small channel can reach. Shorts-only ad revenue is not a viable passive income target for a new channel.
- A Spanish-language channel aimed at US Hispanic viewers could plausibly reach mid-single-digit RPMs, but the only RPM numbers are vendor guesses. A LatAm-heavy audience could cut RPM to $1 to $3, which roughly triples the views needed.
- Survivorship bias is severe. Every income number found comes from winners (Kapwing's top channels, Flippa sellers). No source gives a defensible median for new faceless channels. Plan for the realistic case of $0 for 6 to 12 months while the channel builds to YPP thresholds.

### Gaps
- No verified, dated study of faceless channel failure rates or time to monetization. All circulating figures trace to SEO blogs.
- AIR's full niche tables are images. Finance, Business, Food and Cooking, and Health RPMs were not in the page text.
- No first-party or study-grade Spanish-language RPM data was found.
- A reported TubeBuddy case (Jan 2026) of $32 from 1M Shorts views (about $0.03 RPM) appeared in search summaries, but the source URL wasn't captured, so it's unverified.

## 3. TikTok Creator Rewards, TikTok Shop affiliate, and TikTok's AI-content rules

### Takeaway
Creator Rewards pays roughly $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (secondary estimates) on original videos over 1 minute from accounts with 10K+ followers. It's better than YouTube Shorts per view but still tiny. TikTok Shop affiliate is the bigger money: US TikTok Shop sales were $15.8B in 2025, and commissions are often 10 to 20%. But TikTok Shop's May 2026 quality rules and brand pushback make purely AI-made product videos risky, and still-image or AI-voice formats are explicitly non-compliant in some contexts.

### Cited Findings
**Creator Rewards Program (CRP), secondary because the official TikTok pages would not load**
- Eligibility: 18+, personal account (not business or political), 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days. Only original videos longer than 1 minute qualify. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/tiktok-creativity-program/); [ttcalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/); [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/passionfruit/tiktok-creator-rewards-program)
- RPM: most 2026 sources say about $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. One table lists US $0.85, UK $0.70, Germany $0.62. One source claims US RPM rose to $0.80 to $1.20 in Jan 2026, about 30% over Q4 2025. TikTok doesn't publish an RPM, so all of these are creator-reported. — [ttcalculator payout update 2026](https://ttcalculator.net/blog/creator-rewards-payout-update-2026/); [VideoDubber](https://videodubber.ai/blogs/how-much-does-tiktok-pay/)
- Disqualifications: creators report videos rejected as "unoriginal" or "low quality" without warning. TikTok's low-quality definition reportedly includes "split-screen videos, irrelevant reactions, slides format, random shots, Q&A format, screen recordings or black screens." Mass-produced slideshows with generic AI voices reportedly get flagged. — [Tech Issues Today](https://techissuestoday.com/tiktok-creators-cry-foul-as-viral-videos-get-disqualified-before-payday); [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/tiktok-slideshow-unoriginal-content-flag)
- I couldn't confirm a TikTok statement that AI-generated content is ineligible for CRP, and sources conflict on this. — [Clippie](https://clippie.ai/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-faceless-ai-videos); [ReelMind](https://reelmind.ai/blogs/can-you-make-money-posting-ai-videos-on-tiktok-and-youtube-shorts-in-2026-curren)

**TikTok Shop affiliate**
- US TikTok Shop sales were $15.82B in 2025, up 108% year on year (2024 growth was 407%), making up 18.2% of US social commerce. eMarketer (Dec 2025) forecasts more than $20B for 2026 and 53.2M US buyers in 2025, rising to 57.7M in 2026. — [Syncly, citing eMarketer](https://syncly.app/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-roi-in-what-the-data-actually-shows)
- Sellers set commission anywhere from 1% to 80% under both the Open and Targeted plans. Targeted rates override Open rates. Rate changes take 30 days to apply on Open and 5 days on Targeted. — [Syncly, citing TikTok Seller University](https://syncly.app/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-roi-in-what-the-data-actually-shows)
- Typical rates (vendor): US average 13.02% in 2026; Open Plan 10 to 15%; Targeted 15 to 25%. TikTok's own seller referral fee is about 6% and is separate. Commissions are clawed back on returns. — [Hamster Garage](https://www.hamstergarage.com/article/tiktok-shop-affiliate-statistics-benchmarks-roi); [Dashboardly, 23 Feb 2026](https://dashboardly.io/post/tiktok-shop-affiliate-commissions-2026-payouts-clawbacks-profit-math)
- Syncly flags as unsupported several widely repeated stats: "affiliates drive 42% of GMV," "mid-tier creators earn a $680/month median," "top 0.5% drive 38% of GMV," and the ROAS benchmarks. — [Syncly](https://syncly.app/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-roi-in-what-the-data-actually-shows). One site's "$215 median monthly earnings" has no disclosed method. — [Dashboardly statistics](https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-affiliate-creator-statistics)
- US affiliate entry: sources conflict on 1,000 vs 5,000 followers. A guide marked "verified July 2026" says US creators under 5,000 followers enter a 30-day Creator Pilot Program limited to shops that meet a performance threshold. Requirements are 18+, US-based and in good standing. Sellers approve free samples, which can come with posting conditions. — [CreatorsAgency](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-requirements-2026); [Hubfluence](https://www.hubfluence.io/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-under-5000-followers)
- TikTok Shop US Academy, "Requirements for High-Quality Videos and LIVEs" (published 23 May 2026): in LIVEs, AI-generated voices and pre-recorded narration replacing a live speaker are banned, and still-frame content may not cover more than 50% of the screen. Shoppable videos made of still images throughout, without the creator's face or the product, are non-compliant, and compliant videos need at least 3 seconds of dynamic content. Only TikTok-library music is allowed. Penalties include violation points, commission restrictions and account restriction, tracked through the Creator Health Rating. PPC Land notes that "AI narration is prohibited for affiliate promotions." — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/?p=19132)
- Brands pushing back (Jul 2026, WSJ-reported memo): a brand told affiliates that an AI tool is barred under its affiliate program's "No AI-Generated Content" policy. Other coverage says TikTok allows disclosed, accurate AI affiliate content, with the "contains AI-generated media" label appearing next to "eligible for commission." — [Newswav, summarizing WSJ](https://newswav.com/article/brands-urge-tiktok-shop-creators-to-ditch-ai-fakes-report-says-A2607_sFmfB6); [Affiverse](https://www.affiversemedia.com/?p=109231); [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/ai-reshapes-tiktok-shop-affiliate-playbook)

**TikTok AI labeling (secondary)**
- TikTok requires labels on realistic AI-generated images, audio and video, encourages labels on fully or significantly AI-edited content, and auto-labels content carrying C2PA Content Credentials. An auto-applied label can't be removed. Deceptive synthetic media and deepfakes are removed and can earn strikes, while labeled AI content is generally allowed. — [Cinerads](https://www.cinerads.com/blog/tiktok-ai-content-policy); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-officially-launches-new-in-stream-labels-for-ai-generated-content/694154/)
- A vendor page claims 1.3B+ TikTok videos have been labeled and that enforcement rose 340% over 2024. This is unverified. — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/statistics/tiktok-ai-content-statistics)

### Inferences
- At $0.40 to $1.00 RPM, $500 a month from CRP takes roughly 0.5M to 1.25M qualified views a month, and $2,000 takes 2M to 5M. That requires an already established 10K+ follower account.
- TikTok Shop affiliate pays per sale, not per view. A $30 product at 15% commission earns $4.50 per sale before returns, so $2,000 a month is about 450 net sales. This is arithmetic, not a benchmark.
- For Brandon, TikTok Shop is better used as a service market, making videos for sellers, than as a faceless AI affiliate channel. The May 2026 rules (no AI voice, real dynamic footage, product on screen) favor real hands-on product footage, which he can shoot. Purely Higgsfield-made affiliate videos carry policy risk and brand pushback.

### Gaps
- Official CRP terms on AI content, US RPM and current eligibility could not be loaded (the TikTok Creator Academy and Support pages returned empty shells).
- No credible median earnings figure for TikTok Shop affiliates. Every circulating median traces to undisclosed SEO data.
- The current official US affiliate follower minimum (1K vs 5K plus pilot) is unconfirmed.

## 4. Instagram and Facebook monetization, Pinterest monetization and affiliate links, and their AI-content policies

### Takeaway
Facebook paid creators nearly $3B in 2025, 60% of it for Reels, through an invite-only Content Monetization program with no rate card. Meta penalizes repeat reposters with lost monetization and reduced reach. Facebook's Creator Fast Track guarantees ($1K to $3K a month) need 100K+ followers elsewhere. Pinterest pays creators almost nothing directly. Its value is affiliate and Amazon links plus traffic, and it now labels AI images and lets users see fewer of them.

### Cited Findings
- Facebook 2025 payouts (primary, Meta, 18 Mar 2026): nearly $3B paid to creators, up 35% from 2024; 60% of payouts went to Reels; creators earning more than $10K a year grew 30%+ year on year. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/)
- Creator Fast Track (announced 18 Mar 2026): three months of guaranteed pay, $1,000 a month for creators with 100K+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, or $3,000 a month for creators with more than 1M on one platform, plus immediate Content Monetization access. A TechCrunch summary reports a requirement of 15 Reels in 30 days spread over 10+ days. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/); [Pulse/TechCrunch summary](https://www.pulse.bot/entertainment/news/facebook-launches-a-new-monetization-program-to-attract-popular-creators-from-tiktok-youtube-5a32b10e-e08d-4163-b363-f8434cd12ffc)
- Facebook Content Monetization is invite-only. ## 5. How automated posting can be: APIs, schedulers, anti-automation rules, and per-video production cost

### Takeaway
All three big platforms have official posting APIs, so a Claude-run pipeline can post legitimately. TikTok's API limits unaudited apps to private posts, and Instagram allows about 100 API posts per 24 hours. The real limit is policy, not tooling: templated, high-volume AI uploads are what YouTube, TikTok and Meta demonetize. AI generation costs per Short range from under $1 (bundled credits) to about $2 (Kling) and $10 to $15 (Veo API) before retries.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Content Posting API (primary): direct posting needs a registered app with the `video.publish` scope approved, and "All content posted by unaudited clients will be restricted to private viewing mode" until the client passes an audit for Terms of Service compliance. The example shows a 300-second maximum video duration. — [TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-get-started)
- Instagram Content Publishing API (primary): publishes images (JPEG only), videos, Reels (including trial Reels), Stories and carousels of up to 20 items to professional accounts. The limit is 100 API-published posts per rolling 24 hours, though one section of the same doc says 50. Shopping tags aren't supported. — [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/)
- YouTube Data API `videos.insert` (primary, read 2026-11-04): the fetch summary says the page lists a cost of "1 unit in the Video Uploads quota bucket," "100 calls per day," and that "Videos uploaded from unverified API projects are not restricted to private viewing mode." This conflicts with the long-standing rule that unverified projects created after 28 Jul 2020 upload as private, so verify it before relying on it. — [YouTube Data API: videos.insert](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert)
- YouTube enforces monetization at channel level, so automating uploads of templated content risks the whole channel. — [PPC Land, Jul 2025](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/)
- TikTok Shop penalizes AI voice and still-frame content in LIVEs and still-image-only shoppable videos (23 May 2026). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/)
- AI generation costs (vendor sources, 2026): Kling about $0.06 to $0.07 per second; Veo $0.40 to $0.50 per second through the API; ElevenLabs voice about $0.02 per second. Per finished 30-second Short including revisions, $5 to $50. Monthly tool stacks for a faceless channel cost $20 to $150. — [FluxNote model pricing](https://fluxnote.io/es-MX/guides/ai-video-model-pricing-comparison-2026); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/toryreut/everyones-generating-videos-i-calculated-what-ai-video-actually-costs-in-2026-37ag); [MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/ai-filmmaking-cost-breakdown-2026/); [FluxNote cost guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/faceless-youtube-channel-cost)

### Inferences
- Rough per-video cost: a 30-second Short with 4 to 6 AI clips plus voice costs about $2 to $5 on Kling-class models, about $10 to $15 on Veo-class. A 10-minute long-form explainer using mostly stills plus a few motion clips might cost $10 to $40 in generation. These are estimates from the per-second prices, not measured, and Brandon's Higgsfield Ultra credits change the math because he's already paying for them.
- At Shorts RPMs of $0.07 to $0.33, a $3 Short needs about 9K to 43K views just to cover generation cost, and pays nothing at all below the Feb 2027 10M per 90 days floor. Long-form at $5 to $15 RPM recovers a $30 video at 2K to 6K views.
- The automation that fits the policies: Claude researches, scripts and drafts. Higgsfield generates B-roll. Brandon reviews and adds voice or face. Posting is scheduled through official APIs or the Higgsfield TikTok publishing tools at human-like volume (for example 3 to 5 long-form videos a week, not 50 Shorts a day).

### Gaps
- No platform publishes a numeric "too many uploads" threshold. The anti-spam limits are policy judgments.
- Whether the Higgsfield connector's TikTok publishing goes through an audited app (public posting) was not checked.
- No measured data on AI-generation retry and failure rates, which multiply raw per-second costs.

## 6. UGC and product-video angles that fit Brandon's skills, and whether Spanish-language faceless channels are less saturated

### Takeaway
Selling videos to brands pays per deliverable and up front, from Billo's $99 entry level to commonly quoted $200 to $1,200 professional rates (vendor sources). That suits Brandon's existing skills better than speculative affiliate or ad-share income. The "Spanish is less saturated" claim is unsupported: Kapwing's data shows Spanish-language AI slop channels are among the largest in the world.

### Cited Findings
- UGC rates (vendor, 2026): overall range $200 to $3,000 per piece, with most professional UGC creators charging $500 to $1,200 per video. — [JoinBrands](https://joinbrands.com/blog/ugc-creator-rates/). Beginner tiers run $75 to $200 per video and professional tiers $1,000 to $3,000+. — [Fueler](https://www.fueler.io/blog/how-much-do-ugc-creators-make-in-the-us-data). Billo starts at $99 per video. — [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/billo-vs-insense/)
- Brands are pushing back on AI fakes in TikTok Shop affiliate content. At least one brand's affiliate program has a "No AI-Generated Content" policy (Jul 2026, WSJ via Newswav). — [Newswav](https://newswav.com/article/brands-urge-tiktok-shop-creators-to-ditch-ai-fakes-report-says-A2607_sFmfB6)
- Spanish-language AI channels at scale (Kapwing, Oct 2025 data): Spain had 8 AI slop channels among its top 100 trending channels, with 20.22M subscribers and 2.52B views. Cuentos Facinantes, a US-based Spanish-language AI channel, had about 1.28B views and 5.95M subscribers. Imperio de Jesús (Spain) had 5.87M subscribers, and two more Spanish channels had 3.5M+ each. The US total was 9 channels, 14.47M subscribers and 3.39B views. — [Kapwing, 28 Nov 2025](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)
- Claims that Spanish faceless content is "underserved" or "lower competition" come from tool and translation vendors with no channel-count data. — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/spanish-language-youtube-channel-guide); [AIR Translate](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/5-reasons-to-translate-your-content-from-english-to-spanish)
- Spanish-speaking YouTube users in Latin America and Spain number about 269.5M, per Statista as cited by a translation vendor (Feb 2024, likely stale). — [AIR Translate](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/5-reasons-to-translate-your-content-from-english-to-spanish)
- Spanish-language sponsorships from brands targeting LatAm or US Hispanics reportedly pay about the same as English deals at the same audience size (vendor claim). — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/spanish-language-youtube-channel-guide)

### Inferences
- The strongest fit for Brandon is selling bilingual (English and Spanish) product and UGC-style videos to sellers and local businesses, paid per video, with honest AI disclosure. He can also add real hands-on footage, which TikTok Shop's rules now favor. This is service income, not passive, but it's predictable and builds on his food-truck ad-pack plan.
- Spanish-language AI story, religious and brainrot formats are already crowded by very large AI operations. A Spanish channel would need a specific, useful niche, such as a bilingual local food guide for Central Florida or explainers for US Hispanic small-business owners, rather than generic AI stories. Its RPM depends on keeping the audience US-based.
- A semi-automated channel could double as Brandon's portfolio, for example a bilingual "Central Florida food truck spotlight" series. It would earn little ad revenue at first but would market his main service. Business owners would have to agree before their footage is used, consistent with his rule on samples.

### Gaps
- No independent dataset counts Spanish-language faceless channels or compares their competition or growth with English ones.
- No verified per-video earnings data from UGC marketplaces. All rate figures come from platforms or vendors with a stake. A reported Pitchlo dataset (645 briefs, Dec 2025 to Oct 2026, median offer of $100 per video) appeared in search summaries, but its URL wasn't captured, so it's unverified.
- Whether AI-generated (Higgsfield) UGC is accepted on Billo, Insense or JoinBrands, or by brands generally, was not checked.
