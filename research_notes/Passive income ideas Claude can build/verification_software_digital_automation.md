# Verification: software/micro-SaaS, digital products/e-commerce, automation/legal/scams notes

Checked 2026-11-04 against `software_tools_microsaas.md`, `digital_products_ecommerce.md` and `automation_reality_legal_scams.md`. The notes files were not edited.

Method: I picked the claims a decision would rest on and tried to refute each one from primary sources: official pricing and policy pages, IRS, FTC, CBP, platform docs and original studies. All pages were fetched on 2026-11-04 unless noted. Related claims are grouped into one row.

Budget note: WebSearch hit its shared per-session cap after about 12 searches, so everything after that comes from about 40 direct page fetches. These pages were blocked again: Etsy (403), Sunbiz (403) and BigIdeasDB (429). Rows that depend on them stay UNVERIFIED.

Verdict key:
- **CONFIRMED**: a primary source, or the original study, says the same thing.
- **CORRECTED**: the source says something materially different.
- **CONFLICTING**: sources disagree with each other.
- **UNVERIFIED**: I found no primary support. This is the default.

## Most important corrections (ordered by how much they change Brandon's decisions)

1. **Vercel Hobby rules out more than AdSense** (S6). Vercel's commercial-use definition includes "advertising the sale of a product or service". So a free tool built as a **lead magnet for elier.visuals** is commercial too and can't sit on Hobby. Either default to Cloudflare static hosting or budget $20/mo for Vercel Pro. Cloudflare's own terms on ads were still not checked.
2. **Lemon Squeezy is winding toward Stripe Managed Payments** (S9). Its CEO's Jan 28, 2026 post confirms this first-hand and admits slower support. Gumroad's merchant-of-record status is confirmed on Gumroad's own page (D3). For a first digital product, Gumroad (simplest, MoR), Paddle or Stripe direct are safer starting points than a new Lemon Squeezy account.
3. **Stripe Tax isn't always 0.5%** (S8). For API integrations it's **$0.50 per transaction**, which is about 5.6% of a $9 sale. If Claude builds a custom checkout that uses Stripe Tax, low-ticket pricing has to account for it. Otherwise use Stripe Checkout or Payment Links (0.5%) or a merchant of record.
4. **Don't plan on the TikTok Shop 8% fee or the Merch on Demand tier dollars** (D10, D11). Neither has a TikTok or Amazon primary source. A reported TikTok 8% increase in 2024 didn't stick. Neither changes the notes' conclusion that both are poor passive bets.
