# Skip trend sites, build bilingual food-business tools

Truly hands-off income is rare, and the three ideas you named are among the weakest bets of late 2026: AI answers roughly halve Google clicks to an AdSense site, Amazon pays about $1 on a $30 item and $0 on sales from ads you buy that link to Amazon, and the median new app earns about $72 a month after a year.

- **Do this:** one bilingual website that also carries your ad-pack page. Part one is free tools plus $19–$39 promo-template kits for food-truck and restaurant owners, the most passive piece once built. Part two is a Central Florida food-truck guide that doubles as a sales tool for your ad packs.
- **Expect (both parts together, my estimates):** typically $0–120 a month at six months and $20–200 at twelve; in a good case, $180–500 and then $400–1,000. The typical US side hustle makes a **median $200 a month**, and one $249-a-month ad client beats the typical month-12 total of both parts.
- **Costs:** about $10–20 a year for a domain (price not checked) and about $90 of county and business-name paperwork if your home is in unincorporated Polk County (another county, a city receipt or a newspaper notice changes that), then about $1–2 a month.
- **Your hours:** 3–5 a week on the site, capped at 5, on top of paid ad-pack orders. Claude builds, writes, checks and reports on a schedule. You open accounts, approve everything before it goes public, film, sell, handle payouts and set aside 25–30% of profit for taxes.

## Short answers to your questions

1. **What works and what's hype?** These work, in a modest way: small tools and template kits for a niche you know, a local guide with real footage, and selling your video skills. These are weak or hype: AI-written trend sites, one-product Amazon pages, mass-posting faceless AI channels, automated Etsy or print shops, and "done-for-you AI stores", which are often outright scams.
2. **What's trending that fits you?** Short spikes: holiday shopping (to December 31) and skincare and supplements on TikTok Shop. Slower trends worth building on: restaurants trying AI, GLP-1 eating, Spanish-speaking Central Florida and Orlando tourism. Live trend charts couldn't be reached for this research, so check them yourself before acting.
3. **What does it cost and earn?** Most options cost under $100 to start. Typical earnings are near zero for the first six months. The comparison table below gives typical and good cases for ten options.
4. **How long until the first dollar?** My estimates: a template kit can sell in 2–6 weeks, and a local guide's first lead takes about 1–3 months. An ad-supported site takes 4–8 months or more, and a YouTube channel 6–12 months or more, if it ever qualifies for pay.
5. **How much can Claude automate?** Building, writing, translating, checking, web-search trend scans and weekly reports; the research's rough guess is that this cuts your hours to about a third. You handle accounts, ID checks, approvals, filming, selling, payouts and taxes.
6. **What are the risks?** Platforms banning mass-made AI content, Google updates and AI answers taking clicks, Amazon's new rules, scams aimed at people looking for exactly this, and taxes on whatever you earn.
7. **What should you do first?** This week, start the shared site with the ad-pack page, the price calculator and the Holiday Pack (one day-by-day list for both plans is below). Collect guide listings on your truck-park visits and save spotlight videos for January. Skip the holiday affiliate test unless ad-pack work leaves you 3–5 spare hours a week.

## Your three ideas are the weakest bets right now

Each idea you described works for a few people. The 2026 data shows each one now fights a strong headwind, and the headwind is strongest exactly where Claude would save you the most work.

### AI answers cut Google clicks roughly in half

AdSense is Google's program that pays a website to show ads. Earnings are usually measured per 1,000 page views, a figure called RPM. The catch is volume. One 15-year-old PDF tool site with verified AdSense data made **$1,197 a month profit on about 197,000 page views a month** ([Flippa listing](https://flippa.com/10100985-online-pdf-conversion-site-adsense-fully-automated-100-html)), and its AdSense revenue works out to about $5.30 per 1,000 views. At that rate, **$100 a month takes about 19,000 page views a month and $1,000 takes about 190,000**. A younger site shows what year one looks like: 30 free calculators, six months old, **19,554 page views a month, a $50-a-month loss, and 4–7 hours a week of upkeep** ([Flippa listing](https://flippa.com/es/13841260-30-free-calculator-and-utility-tools-across-8-niches-finance-health-construction-seo-youtube-ai-math-insta-business-seo-traffic-adsense-monetized)).

Getting those views from Google gets harder every year. In Pew's study of real browsing, people clicked a normal result on **8% of searches that showed an AI summary, against 15% without one**, and only 1% clicked a link inside the summary ([Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)). Ahrefs measured a **58% drop in clicks for the #1 result** when an AI Overview (Google's AI answer box) appears; Ahrefs calls this a correlation, and click rates fell even on keywords without one ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update)). Chartbeat data show Google search traffic to US publishers fell 38% in the year to November 2025 ([Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/publishers/digital-journalism/google-traffic-down-2025-trends-report-2026/)), and small publishers reportedly lost about 60% over one to two years (sources differ on the window) ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/17/chartbeat-search-traffic-ai-chatbots)). Sales of traditional content businesses on Flippa fell 39% in the first half of 2026 ([Flippa](https://flippa.com/blog/digital-ma-insights-h1-2026/)).

The "trending topic" part makes it worse. Only **1.74% of new pages reached Google's top 10 within a year** (6.11% in a cleaner English sample; both 2023 data, from before AI Overviews), and the average #1 page is five years old ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank/)). A topic that trends this week is over before a new page ranks. The "Claude writes 100 pages a night" version is the exact pattern Google names as spam: "using generative AI tools… to generate many pages without adding value for users" is scaled content abuse ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)). One site that published 850,000 AI "local news" pages surged in March 2026, then got a manual penalty that removed the section from Google and from Google's AI answers ([GSQI](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/when-mt-ai-crumbles-chatgpt-follows/)). Google also tells publishers to fact-check all AI content by hand before it goes live ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content)). AdSense works as a small extra on a site people already visit for another reason, not as the plan.

### Amazon pays about $1 on a $30 item, and nothing on sales from ads you buy that link to it

Amazon Associates pays a commission when someone buys through your link. Its public US rates are **3% for Home, Furniture, Toys, Beauty, Pets and Sports, 4.5% for Kitchen, 1% for Grocery and Health & Personal Care, and 10% only for luxury beauty categories** ([Amazon](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ)). A $30 home or kitchen item pays $0.90–$1.35, so **$100 a month takes about 75–110 sales and $1,000 takes about 740–1,100**. Some publishers, mostly paid-media sites, reported their negotiated rates cut by up to half in 2026 ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/amazon-associates-affiliate-rate-cuts-publishers/)). Amazon reportedly disputes how widely that applied, and new small affiliates are paid from the public rates anyway.

Since **April 14, 2026**, Amazon's agreement disqualifies any purchase by a customer referred "through any advertisement that you purchased", and Amazon's change log says this covers boosted posts too ([Amazon Operating Agreement changes](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/compare)). So boosting a TikTok or running ads that link to Amazon earns **$0**. The only carve-out the research found is paid search that sends people to your own page first; paid social to your own page isn't covered. The same update adds an "original content" test: content that uses outside material "must contain significant commentary, analysis, or transformation".

Timing kills the "popping product" idea. In a Publicis–TikTok study of 1,000+ hashtags, mostly in Asia-Pacific markets plus the US, **48% of TikTok trends faded within five days, and only 26.7% lasted beyond two weeks** ([Campaign Asia](https://www.campaignasia.com/article/publicis-and-tiktok-crack-the-code-on-why-only-27-trends-survive-beyond-two-weeks/4bj3k6n8htrywyqvppeyk43j5r)). Viral products decay even while they sell. NeoCell collagen fell about 33% from one quarter to the next while still in TikTok Shop's top 10 ([FastMoss](https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/best-selling-tiktok-shop-products-us-q2-2026/)), and Labubu resale prices fell 50%+ in some cases within months of the peak, partly as Pop Mart raised supply about tenfold ([Inside Retail](https://insideretail.co.nz/2025/09/23/has-the-labubu-craze-reached-its-peak-trend-experts-discuss-the-drop-in-demand/)). A new page can't rank in five days, so every visitor has to come from your own videos. The YouTuber's model is really "make viral short videos and send people to a link". That's a video job, which suits you, but it isn't passive. The honest version is the optional holiday test in the plan below.

### The median new app earns $72 a month after a year

You're right that Claude can write software, and that's exactly why code alone is now worth so little. New subscription-app launches rose to **14,700+ a month by January 2026**. One year after launch, the median app makes **about $72 a month**; the top quarter make more than $429 and the top 10% more than $2,574. Only **17.3% reach $1,000 a month within two years** ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/)). In your own niche, GrubLedger, a food-truck app with permit tracking, event booking and live card billing, had **zero subscribers after five months and was listed for sale at $300** ([Flippa listing](https://flippa.com/13529765-turnkey-saas-for-food-truck-operators-permit-tracking-event-booking-prep-lists-catering-quotes-financials-stripe-billing-live-with-3-tiers-free-trial)).

Other routes look no better. AI tools priced under $50 a month keep only **23% of their revenue after a year**, even among established products with $250k+ in yearly revenue ([ChartMogul](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)). One developer's 38 Chrome extensions made $31 a month after six months ([DEV](https://dev.to/ktg0215/real-numbers-freemium-chrome-extension-monetization-after-6-months-5hga)). Apple charges $99 a year ([Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/)) and rejects apps that are "not particularly useful, unique, or 'app-like'" ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). The scarce thing is customers, not code. Your restaurant ad-app plan is already your software bet, so don't start a second subscription product. The site below should feed that one.

### Door 2: a Central Florida food-truck guide with spotlight videos

This is an evergreen bilingual guide for diners and visitors: one page per truck around Four Corners, Davenport and Kissimmee, plus pages for regular truck parks and events. Trucks join through a **"Get listed free / Aparece gratis" form** (a free Tally form behind a QR code you hand out), which records the truck's own details, its consent and a contact. ### Optional: an eight-week holiday affiliate test, organic only

**Default: skip it.** Do it only if ad-pack work leaves you 3–5 spare hours a week. This is the honest version of the Amazon idea. First check your TikTok follower count: sources disagree on whether US TikTok Shop affiliates need 1,000 or 5,000 followers. If you qualify, use TikTok Shop. New Amazon applicants have 180 days to make 3 qualifying sales, or the application can be withdrawn ([Amazon help](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG)).

Six rules apply:

1. Don't boost posts or run ads to Amazon; that pays $0.
2. Put "#ad" and "Paid link / Enlace pagado" in the video itself, in the language you speak.
3. Show Amazon's required line, "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases", on your profile or link page, with a Spanish version beside it (Amazon's standard wording; not re-checked here).
4. Register each social account or link page as a Site in Associates Central and use direct Amazon links. Amazon disqualifies redirects through an intermediate site and bans shorteners that hide the destination ([Amazon policies](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies)).
5. No AI voices or still-image videos. TikTok Shop's May 2026 rules ban AI voices in Shop LIVEs and still-image-only shoppable videos ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/)), and at least one brand bans AI-generated content from its affiliate program ([Newswav](https://newswav.com/article/brands-urge-tiktok-shop-creators-to-ditch-ai-fakes-report-says-A2607_sFmfB6)).
6. No prices on a page unless they come from Amazon's Creators API with a time stamp; the old product-data tool is shut off ([Amazon](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/creatorsapi/docs/en-us/paapiv5-deprecation)).

**Kill rule:** if commissions total under $50 by December 31, stop, and use what you learned about which videos sell as proof for your UGC pitches.

## Bans, Google updates, scams and taxes can zero out a passive project

The biggest risk to any passive project isn't that Claude can't build it. It's that a platform switches it off, or that the money gets eaten by a scam or a tax bill.

### Platforms punish mass-produced AI content, sometimes by mistake

Every traffic source you'd use has removed accounts in bulk in the last two years. In December 2025, YouTube terminated two AI fake-movie-trailer channels with a combined 2M+ subscribers ([Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/12/26/youtube-ai-film-trailers-takedowns/)). In January 2026 it reportedly removed 16 AI channels with about 35 million subscribers, according to figures from Kapwing and The Next Web that YouTube didn't confirm ([Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2589917/youtube-deletes-47-billion-views-worth-of-ai-spam-videos-in-major-platform-crackdown); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)). Pinterest wrongly mass-suspended accounts in spring 2025 and admitted an "internal error" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/14/pinterest-finally-admits-mass-bans-were-a-mistake-caused-by-an-internal-error/)). Google can drop a whole section of a site with one manual penalty (above). None of these platforms bans AI-made content outright. What they punish is volume, sameness, hiding the AI and fake people. YouTube says honestly labeled AI content doesn't lose reach or pay ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)). Your existing rules (tell businesses samples are AI-made, never show AI people as real customers) already match all of this. Keep your own website and an email list as a fallback, so one ban can't wipe you out.

### Scam red flags

"Passive income with AI" is one of the most-used pitches in recent FTC fraud cases. In 2024, business and job-opportunity scams were the third most-reported fraud category, with **$750.6M in reported losses** ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/03/new-ftc-data-show-big-jump-reported-losses-fraud-125-billion-2024)). Walk away if you see any of these:

1. **A big upfront fee for a "done-for-you", "AI-powered" or "automated" store, channel or app.** Click Profit, sued in South Florida, charged management fees of at least $45,000, and the FTC alleges Amazon shut down about 95% of its stores ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/03/ftc-acts-stop-click-profit-online-business-opportunity-has-cost-consumers-least-14-million)).
2. **Income promises or guarantees.** Growth Cave promised "$20,000 to $50,000 in passive income", said it was "IMPOSSIBLE to fail" and offered a $10,000 profit guarantee. The FTC says it took about $50M ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/03/ftc-takes-action-stop-sprawling-growth-cave-business-opportunity-credit-repair-scam)).
3. **Testimonials from actors or staff, or lifestyle shots** (cash, cars). Growth Cave's testimonials were undisclosed employees (same source).
4. **They run the store on your Amazon or Walmart account.** In the Automators AI case, platforms "routinely suspended, blocked, or terminated" those stores ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/02/ftc-action-leads-ban-owners-automators-ai-e-commerce-money-making-scheme)).
5. **Claimed ties to big brands, or "buyout" promises.** The FTC alleges Click Profit implied ties to Nike and Disney and talked of buyouts "at a 3-6x multiple" (FTC, above).
6. **Upsell ladders and rebrands.** Growth Cave's $30,000–$50,000 "mastermind" add-on, and an "AI" app that recycled old testimonials (FTC, above).
7. **Gag clauses and pressure over reviews.** The FTC alleges Click Profit threatened customers who complained with lawsuits (FTC, above), and Ascend Ecom pushed buyers to change negative reviews ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-announces-crackdown-deceptive-ai-claims-schemes)).
8. **Spanish-language pitches with contracts often in English.** That was the Ganadores pattern, from a Florida coaching scheme aimed at Spanish speakers ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/06/ftc-acts-stop-real-estate-online-commerce-coaching-scheme-ganadores-targeting-spanish-speaking)).
9. **YouTube ads plus income screenshots.** Screenshots aren't proof. Ask for numbers a third party verifies, such as listings with data checked by Flippa or revenue dashboards connected to Stripe.

The same rules apply if you ever sell. The FTC says any earnings claim must reflect what a typical buyer achieves, and a "results not typical" note isn't enough ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guidance-concerning-multi-level-marketing)). So your kits promise better-looking posts, never income.

### Florida legal and tax: about $90 of paperwork, and 25–30% set aside

You don't need an LLC. A single-member LLC is taxed exactly like a sole proprietor, on Schedule C ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies)). It mainly adds liability protection for client work, and its filing fees (about $125 to form and $138.75 a year) weren't confirmed on Sunbiz, which was blocked ([Sunbiz](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/forms/fees/)). If you trade as "elier.visuals" without an LLC, register it as a fictitious name, about $50 for five years (also not confirmed). Florida may also require a one-time newspaper notice of the name before you register; that wasn't checked, so confirm it on Sunbiz first. If your home is in Polk County, a Class A business tax receipt costs $39.38 a year and renews by September 30 ([Polk Tax Collector](https://www.polktaxes.com/services/local-business-taxes/)). City receipts are separate, so a home inside Davenport city limits likely needs one too (not checked). Four Corners spans Polk, Osceola, Lake and Orange, and the other counties' fees weren't researched. A free EIN keeps your Social Security number off payers' tax forms.

On federal tax, self-employment tax is **15.3%** ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes)), and you must file once net self-employment earnings reach $400 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employed-individuals-tax-center)). That means $3,000 of profit owes about $424 in self-employment tax before income tax. Florida has no state income tax. If you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year (the general IRS rule, not verified here), pay estimated tax by April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/estimated-tax/individuals/individuals-2)). The 25–30% set-aside is my rule of thumb from these rates; confirm it with a tax pro. Payment apps now report you only above $20,000 *and* 200 transactions ([IRS](https://irs.gov/newsroom/irs-issues-faqs-on-form-1099-k-threshold-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-dollar-limit-reverts-to-20000)), and payers file a 1099-NEC only from $2,000 for 2026 payments ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i1099mec.pdf)). You may get no tax forms at all, but every dollar is still taxable. AdSense also needs your tax info and an identity check before it shows ads, mails a PIN once you reach $10, and pays at $100 ([AdSense Help](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709871?hl=en)).

On disclosure, the FTC says "Paid link" next to an affiliate link is adequate, a note on your home page isn't, a video needs the disclosure in the video itself, and Spanish content needs a Spanish disclosure ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides)). Fake or AI-generated reviews and testimonials are banned outright, with penalties of up to **$53,088 per violation** ([Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/ftc-warns-companies-of-potential-violations-of-the-consumer-review-rule)). Florida likely doesn't tax downloaded digital products ([Sales Tax Handbook](https://www.salestaxhandbook.com/florida/sales-tax-taxability/software-and-digital-products)), but sources conflict, and selling through Gumroad as merchant of record puts sales tax on Gumroad anyway. Write your kit pages and refund policy in both languages, so you never repeat the Ganadores pattern, and make refunds generous: Gumroad may refund within 90 days anyway to head off chargebacks ([Gumroad Help](https://gumroad.com/help/article/51-what-is-gumroads-refund-policy)).

## Conclusion

Across platforms, 2026's rules pay for original work by real people and punish volume, so the part Claude can't fake (your face, your footage, your Spanish, your relationships with owners at the truck park) is what earns. That also changes how to judge success. A passive project for you doesn't need to earn much on its own. One food-truck owner who finds your calculator, buys a $19 pack and then a $249 Monthly Refresh is worth more than a year of AdSense on a trend site. Because the site costs about $1–2 a month and every piece feeds your main business, the downside is a capped few hours a week until February 2, and the good case is a small, growing income that builds your ad business too.
