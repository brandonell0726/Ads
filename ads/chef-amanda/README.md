## How it's built

Hook (ropa vieja, the yolk breaks, "This came out of a FOOD TRUCK.") → their logo over the truck on the beat drop → six dishes, one per bar, each with a pink label like the ones on their menu board → close with the logo, address and "Come hungry."

- `anuncio.json`: the shot plan (shot order, bars per shot, where each clip starts)
- `diseno/`: the text and graphics layer (`capas.html`, `estilo.css`, `animacion.js`)
- `render/index.html`: the finished HyperFrames page that was rendered (assets `a01`–`a08` = clips, `a09` = white logo, `a10` = music)
- `musica.json`: "Mi Gente" by Arulo (Mixkit Stock Music Free License: commercial use OK, credit not required), 110 BPM, starts at 41.389 s
- `notas.md`: dish names and descriptions from their own menu card
