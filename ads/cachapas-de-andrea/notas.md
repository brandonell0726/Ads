## Brand
- Tagline: "Una cachapa nunca había sido tan cool". Also "Desde 2006", "Cachapas 100% maíz", "Las Cachapas de maíz 100% gourmet más famosas de Orlando".
- Logo: navy round badge, gold "CACHAPAS / DE ANDREA", corn cob with green husks, "SINCE 2006".
- Menu tags on their board are orange cards held by binder rings.

## Menu items seen in their own photos and menu board
- Cachapa con queso (½ queso or queso completo), queso de mano.
- Cachapa con carne mechada (their studio photo), cachapa con chicharrón (with lime and nata), cachapa con tajada y queso, cachapa con milanesa de pollo, cachapa with bacon.
- Nata on the side. Drinks include Maltín and sodas, plus an iced drink in their branded cup.
- No prices in the ad.
